# Two Hearts Nourish — quiet intimacy

Design should feel like a place the two people actually return to, not a seasonal Valentine's template.

## Design contract
- **Warm ink / paper / oxblood.** Cream surfaces, muted dusty-rose accent, honey secondary. Dark mode stays warm and legible. No pink gradients, saturated candy controls or decorative glass cards.
- **Typography over illustration.** Fraunces for a single editorial headline per page; Figtree for interface text and measurements. No fake couple photography, fake meals, SVG clip-art hearts or invented relationship milestones.
- **Relationship first, metrics second.** Show the partners' real names and their own shared goal; two-person progress uses actual logs and never infers meals were eaten together.
- **One-tap utility.** The 'Meals you know by heart' flow makes the emotional connection useful: repeat their own meals with the same portion; let them save favourites or choose recents. Never share each other's macros by default.
- **Fewer cards.** Prefer dividers, typographic spacing and compact timelines. Expose the next meaningful action rather than a grid of quick-action pills.
- **Earned affection.** One modest heart mark per screen at most. Avoid cliché motivational quotes, randomly generated praise, emojis in every heading, and pressure to maintain streaks.
- **States matter.** Empty, offline, loading and retry states are explicitly described. Never show a cached result as 'latest' while a refresh is ongoing.
- **Privacy is intimate too.** Recovery and device views communicate exactly whose sign-ins are shown, what is revocable, and whether activity information is real or only a last Settings visit.
