# Our Health Space — product truth

## Register
Operate: a private, everyday nutrition journal for exactly two people, each on their own phone.

## Audience and real-world use
Two partners who eat and log independently, often separated by time zones. They open the app briefly to record meals, reuse their usual portions, check their own targets, and acknowledge the everyday care they share. They may work different hours. The UI must never imply both ate at the same time merely because they logged on the same date.

## Primary tasks
1. One-tap repeat a personally eaten meal with its saved portion.
2. Scan or manually add a new meal and correct its macros.
3. View each partner's separate daily nutrition at a glance.
4. Browse and edit diary entries; adjust their own targets.
5. Reconnect without accidentally creating duplicate identities or devices.

## Nonnegotiables
- Never fabricate foods, dates, calories, personal photos, couple milestones, or partner device access.
- Meals planned but not eaten are excluded from consumed totals.
- A missing nutrition goal never produces a made-up progress percentage.
- Meal photos remain in a private Supabase bucket; signed URLs only.
- Personal devices, keys and permissions do not expose the partner's sign-ins.
- Two profiles remain distinct. Repeat meals only from the caller's history.
- Keep existing scanning, manual logging, favorites, editing, planning, weight logging, recovery and export functional.
- Mobile first, accessible on narrow Android screens and iPhone Safari; support reduced motion and explicit dark mode.
- No new paid service or Lovable AI usage required for the redesign.

## Emotional promise
Warm, quietly affectionate and mature. The product feels like a shared personal notebook, not a Valentine campaign, dating app, wedding website or AI-generated SaaS dashboard.
